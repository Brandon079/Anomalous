# Anomalous Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026\
**Last Updated:** September 23, 2026

Anomalous is an astronomy application designed to analyze astronomical
images, identify cataloged objects, and assist users in investigating
potentially unusual or uncataloged objects.

Your privacy is important. Anomalous is designed so that your images and
analysis data remain under your control.

## Information Collection

Anomalous does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account with Anomalous, and
Anomalous does not operate a server that stores your personal
information, astronomical images, or analysis results.

Images, analysis results, settings, downloaded catalogs, and other
locally generated application data are stored and processed on your
device unless you explicitly choose to use an online service.

## Image Processing

Image analysis performed by Anomalous is performed locally on your
device.

Your astronomical images are not automatically uploaded to Anomalous or
to a server operated by the developer.

Anomalous may provide features that allow you to intentionally send
information or images to third-party services. These features operate
only when you choose to use them.

## Online Plate Solving

Anomalous supports both local plate solving and online plate solving.

When using the local plate solver, plate solving is performed on your
device using locally installed star-index data.

If you choose to use the Astrometry.net online plate-solving service,
image data necessary to perform the plate solve is transmitted to
Astrometry.net. Information transmitted to Astrometry.net is subject to
the privacy practices and terms of that service.

Anomalous does not control how third-party services process information
submitted directly to them.

## Online Catalog and Reference Services

Some Anomalous features may query external astronomical databases and
services, such as SIMBAD, VizieR, sky-survey services, or other
astronomical reference databases.

These requests may contain astronomical information such as celestial
coordinates, search radius, field of view, or object identifiers
necessary to perform the requested lookup.

Your original astronomical image is not uploaded to these catalog
services merely to perform a catalog lookup.

As with any internet connection, the operator of an external service may
receive technical information normally associated with a network
request, such as your IP address.

## AstroBin

Anomalous may allow users to access images or information from AstroBin
when the user chooses to use that feature.

Any information exchanged directly with AstroBin is subject to
AstroBin's own privacy practices and terms.

Anomalous does not collect or store AstroBin account credentials on a
server operated by the developer.

## Local Storage

Anomalous may store information on your device that is necessary for the
operation of the application, including:

-   Application settings and preferences
-   Downloaded astronomical catalogs
-   Plate-solving index files
-   Analysis results and finding history
-   Locally generated reports and exported data
-   Information required for locally operated application features

This information remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to
export, share, or transmit it using a feature that requires an external
service.

### Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by Anomalous remains on your device until you delete
it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Anomalous does not maintain a developer-controlled server containing
copies of this locally stored data.

Files that you intentionally export, save, or share may remain on your
device or with the service to which you shared them until separately
deleted according to the controls and policies of that device or
service.

## Data Sharing and Sale

Anomalous does not sell your personal information.

Anomalous does not sell your astronomical images.

Anomalous does not share your images or locally generated analysis data
with advertisers or data brokers.

Information is transmitted to a third-party service only when necessary
to perform an online feature that you choose to use.

## Advertising

Anomalous does not use user images or analysis results for advertising
purposes.

## Children's Privacy

Anomalous is a general-purpose astronomy application and is not designed
to collect personal information from children.

Because Anomalous does not operate an account system or
developer-controlled service that collects personal information, the
developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children
through the application.

## Data Security

Anomalous minimizes privacy risk by performing image processing and
analysis locally whenever possible and by avoiding unnecessary
collection of user information.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be
guaranteed to be completely secure. When you choose to use an external
online service, information transmitted to that service is subject to
the security and privacy practices of the service provider.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Anomalous gains new features,
changes how information is processed, or integrates additional services.

Any updated policy will include a revised effective date.
